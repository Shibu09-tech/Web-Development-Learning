About Us

At SugarCup, every cupcake is a little work of art crafted with pure love. We are a passion-driven, handmade cupcake bakery that believes in using only the freshest, finest-quality ingredients — because you deserve nothing less. Each batch is baked made-to-order, ensuring you receive cupcakes that are warm, fresh, and bursting with flavour. Whether it's a birthday, anniversary, baby shower, or just a sweet craving — SugarCup is here to make your celebrations extra special, one cupcake at a time. 🧁
